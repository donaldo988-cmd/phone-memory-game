How the game works

There are 5 stages for each player. In every stage, you must guess 2 random digits from your partner's phone number. Correct digits stay saved in your progress. If you clear the full stage, you earn points based on the difficulty you chose: Hard Mode gives 2 points, Easy Mode gives 1 point. First player to reach 10 points wins the race to the prize wheel.

You can also choose to bet your luck for one extra point 👀. That adds 1 extra random digit. If you get it right, you earn the bonus point. If you get it wrong, you lose all points for that round and must face your partner's punishment 😈
